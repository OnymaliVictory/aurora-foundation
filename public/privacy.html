Privacy Policy

Plain-language summary aligned with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023. Have it reviewed before launch.

What we collect. Name, email, phone, role, listings, messages, inspection requests and, for agents, ID type and business details.

Why. To run the service, connect seekers with agents, prevent fraud and meet legal duties. Our lawful bases are contract, legitimate interests (safety and fraud prevention) and consent where required.

Who sees it. Phone numbers are shown only to logged-in users. Verification details are visible only to you and Aurora admins. We do not sell personal data.

Your rights. You may ask to access, correct or delete your data, object to processing or withdraw consent by emailing support@aurora-estates.example. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Security & retention. Data is stored with Supabase using access controls and encryption in transit. We keep data while your account is active and as long as the law requires.
